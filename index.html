How It Works

Get Your Code: Purchase your entry above. You'll receive an email with your unique code and the link to the digital hunt.
Start the Hunt: Head to East Grinstead town centre and begin at the car park opposite the Chequer Mead theatre. The hunt is browser-based (no app needed) and takes 60-90 minutes. The hunt will be open from 3pm on Friday 27th March and the route is suitable for pushchairs, dogs and children.
Solve & Win: Follow the clues to find the final codeword. Submit your entry code and codeword to enter the £100 draw! All entries must be received by 10pm Sunday 12th April.
